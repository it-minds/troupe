Rules

  • One reserved colour. Magenta means a person must decide and nothing else. No branding, links, highlights or warnings in it.
  • The mask stays hollow and mouthless. Two eyes and an outline. A mouth promises a personality the product does not have.
  • People never get a mask. A human is initials in a ring, always.
  • Eyes invert with their half. An eye on a filled half is cut out of it; an eye on a hollow half is solid. Never two solid eyes on a split mask.
  • Status is never colour alone. Eye shape, word, then colour. Greyscale has to survive.
  • Themes swap values, never names. Components read --waiting-solid, not a hex. A theme that needs a new token name is not a theme, it is a redesign.
